### Overall Impression

`index.php` succeeds at honest product communication—its embedded dashboard preview (`index.php:55-162`) uses real vending SKUs and minimum thresholds, and its 1-click live demo path (`login.php?demo=1`) respects how skeptical operators evaluate software. However, the page undermines that domain credibility by wrapping it in every AI-SaaS visual cliché (`gradient-text`, `pulsing-dot`, `overused-font`, rainbow icon boxes, and 4 separate repetitions of the hardware disclaimer) alongside prototype rough edges like inert hover-underlined table actions (`index.php:130,138,146,154`) and browser `alert()` dialogs (`footer.php:52-53`). The single biggest opportunity is to strip away the generic SaaS template decorations and turn the hero preview and feature narrative into an authentic, interactive route-restocking showcase.
